- Title: Udambu
- Card text: The human body at two levels. Explore explains 63 parts in plain words with real 3D models you can turn, eight-second demos of how each works, AR to place a part on your floor, 49 common conditions, a quiz that explains every answer and every line read aloud. The Lab is the student level: 2,827 named structures from open anatomy data, layer by layer, with cross-sections, build-the-body, heartbeat and breathing, spaced-repetition study and life-size VR on Meta Quest. Every Explore part links to the same structure in the Lab. Material 3 on Android, Apple HIG on iPhone.
